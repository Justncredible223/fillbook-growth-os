generated-bed-01 .. generated-bed-24.mp3
----------------------------------------
Synthesised in-house by backend/scripts/video-factory/makeMusicTrack.mjs
(seeds "fillbook-bed-01" .. "fillbook-bed-24", 150 s, loudness-normalised to -14 LUFS).
No third-party audio or samples; no licence restrictions.

Eight styles, cycling by number so consecutive renders always change style:
  01 lofi        mellow, swung beat, electric-piano chords      (72-84 bpm)
  02 drive       driving four-on-the-floor, rolling bass, arp    (120-128 bpm)
  03 piano       sparse piano, no drums, space                   (62-74 bpm)
  04 corporate   bright, bouncy, upbeat                          (104-114 bpm)
  05 synthwave   retro drive, gated chords, bass 8ths            (96-106 bpm)
  06 deephouse   offbeat hats, stabs, side-chained pump          (118-124 bpm)
  07 cinematic   low drone, bells, risers, deep hits             (84-92 bpm)
  08 ambient     soft pad and plucks (the original style)       (82-121 bpm)
(then 09..16 and 17..24 repeat the cycle with different seeds.)

The one third-party track this folder used to hold ("Ambient Technology" by
The_Mountain, Pixabay) was removed 2026-10-03: TikTok's audio matching labelled
videos that used it "Contains: ... The_Mount", which is a flag a business account
does not want. Keep this folder to synthesised beds only.

Add more with (style is one of lofi, drive, piano, corporate, synthwave, deephouse, cinematic, ambient):
  node backend/scripts/video-factory/makeMusicTrack.mjs --seed fillbook-bed-25 --style lofi --duration 150 --out bed.wav
  ffmpeg -i bed.wav -af loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1:LRA=7 -ar 44100 -ac 1 -c:a libmp3lame -b:a 96k backend/scripts/video-factory/assets/music/generated-bed-25.mp3
Any .mp3 in this folder is picked up automatically (see music.ts) and joins the rotation.
